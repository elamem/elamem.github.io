# Delete your My Books account

You can delete your My Books account and its data at any time.

## In the app
1. Open **My Books** and go to **Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type **DELETE** to confirm, then tap **Delete**.

You need an internet connection. When it's done, the app signs you out.

## Without the app
Email **ellog.care@gmail.com** from the email address of your account, with the subject
**"Delete my My Books account"**. We'll delete the account within 30 days and reply to confirm.

## What is deleted
- Your sign-in (email address, name and account ID).
- Your library: books, shelves, tags, cover photos, lending details and reading history, on all
  your devices.
- Your online backups (My Books Pro).

## What is kept
- **Households:** if you are a member of someone else's household, the books you added stay with
  that household, without your name. If you own a household that others have joined, remove the
  other members first (Profile → the ✕ next to each member), then delete your account.
- Copies in our service providers' internal backups are overwritten within a limited time.
  Crash reports (which contain no personal details) are kept for up to 90 days.
- Backup files you exported yourself are stored wherever you saved them; we can't delete those.

See also the [privacy policy](privacy-policy.html).
